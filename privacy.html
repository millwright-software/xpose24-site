What We Collect

Xpose24 is designed with privacy at its core. Here's what we do and don't collect:

✓ What We Keep Local

Your photos (encrypted), preferences, and shot count—stored only on your device.

✗ What We Don't Collect

No personal information, no accounts, no emails, no location data, no advertising ID.

✗ What We Don't Transmit

We never receive your photos. There is no account and no server — nothing is uploaded, no cloud storage, no server logs, no tracking. If you choose to save or share a photo yourself, it goes wherever you send it; that is always your decision, never ours.

✗ No Analytics

We don't use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Firebase, or any third-party analytics SDKs.

Device Permissions

Xpose24 requests the following permissions:

  • Camera Access – Required to capture photos. You must grant this permission for the app to function.
  • Photo Library Access – Optional. Only used when you explicitly choose to save photos to your device's photo library.
  • Face ID / Touch ID (Optional) – If you enable the app lock, Xpose24 uses Apple's on-device biometric authentication. Biometric data is handled entirely by iOS and never leaves your device—Xpose24 never sees or stores it.

You can change or revoke these permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

How Your Photos Are Stored

Local Encryption (On Your Device)

All your photos are encrypted locally using AES-256 (AES-GCM) encryption.

Here's how it works:

  • Encrypted Storage: Full-resolution photos are encrypted and stored securely in your device's app container—a private directory that only Xpose24 can access.
  • Encryption Key: The encryption key is generated uniquely for your device and never leaves your phone. It is not backed up to iCloud or synced anywhere.
  • Your Device, Your Data: If you lose your device, your encrypted photos remain on it. The data cannot be accessed without the encryption key stored on that specific device.

Optional Photo Library Saves

When you choose to save a photo to your device's standard Photo Library:

  • You control whether this happens—we never save automatically
  • Only low-resolution versions are saved (with optional watermark)
  • Full-resolution encrypted versions remain in the app only
  • This gives you freedom to share or backup while protecting your original quality photos

iCloud & Backup

Xpose24 photos are not included in iCloud backups or iCloud Backup. This is intentional: your full-res encrypted photos stay under your direct control, not in Apple's cloud.

Third Parties

Apple's App Store & StoreKit

Xpose24 uses Apple's StoreKit 2 for in-app purchases (film packs, premium features). When you make a purchase:

  • Apple handles all payment processing securely
  • We never see your credit card, Apple ID password, or billing information
  • Apple may share basic transaction data with us (purchase date, item purchased, user ID) as needed to validate your purchase
  • Refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how Apple handles payment data

No Other Third Parties

Beyond Apple StoreKit, Xpose24 does not use:

  • Advertising networks or ad SDKs (no ads, no advertising identifier, no IDFA)
  • Analytics libraries (Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.)
  • Crash reporting services (Crashlytics, Bugsnag, etc.)
  • Social media SDKs
  • Cloud storage services
  • Content delivery networks for your photos

Your app usage and photo data remain yours alone.

System Frameworks

Xpose24 uses only Apple's native frameworks (SwiftUI, AVFoundation, Core Image) for camera, photos, and encryption. These are part of iOS and don't involve external data collection.

Children's Privacy

Age Appropriateness

Xpose24 is suitable for users of all ages, including children. We designed it to encourage mindful photography habits—especially valuable for younger users.

COPPA Compliance

(Children's Online Privacy Protection Act – US regulation)

While Xpose24 is age-appropriate, we want to be transparent about COPPA:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We don't collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personal information—so COPPA's restrictions on collecting children's personal data don't directly apply, because we collect nothing.
  • No Advertising: Xpose24 contains no ads and no advertising SDKs, so no ad-related data is ever collected from children (or anyone else).
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We don't profile, track, or target children (or anyone) based on app usage.
  • Parental Involvement: Parents can monitor app usage, revoke permissions, or delete the app at any time through device settings.

Parental Controls

iOS provides built-in tools for parents:

  • Screen Time: Set app usage limits and schedules
  • Content & Privacy Restrictions: Control which apps can be installed or used
  • Location Services: Manage app permissions (camera, photos, etc.)

These controls are available in Settings → Screen Time on your device.

Your Rights

Data Access & Deletion

Your data is stored only on your device. You have complete control:

  • View: All your photos are visible in the app
  • Delete: Remove photos directly in the app, or uninstall to delete all local data
  • Export: Save photos to your device's Photo Library manually

Because we don't collect or store data on our servers, there's no server-side data to request or delete.

Device-Level Control

  • Camera Permission: You can deny camera access in Settings → Privacy → Camera
  • Photo Library Permission: You can deny photo library access in Settings → Privacy → Photos
  • Uninstall: Removing the app deletes all local Xpose24 data (encrypted files, app preferences, etc.)

No Opt-Out Needed

Because we don't collect personal data, track usage, or use analytics, there's no "opt-out" form to fill. Your privacy is protected by design, not by choice.

  • For significant changes affecting your privacy, we may notify you within the app or via email (if you've provided contact info)
  • Your continued use of Xpose24 after changes means you accept the updated policy

Check back regularly to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.
